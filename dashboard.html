Score Formula / अंक गणना सूत्र
Work Score /25: Current FY Mandays > 0 Works ÷ Total Works × 25
Wage Score /50: Total Wage Booked ÷ Sanctioned Wage × 50
Material Score /25: Total Material Booked ÷ Sanctioned Material × 25
Overall Achievement /100: Work Score + Wage Score + Material Score
Remaining Wage: Wage Sanctioned − Wage Booked
Remaining Material: Material Sanctioned − Material Booked
Data updated: 20-08-2026 | 755 verified works. Score/ranking calculation is kept exactly on the existing dashboard logic. 2025-2026 = NREGA previous-period values; 2026-2027 = VBGRAMG current FY values.
