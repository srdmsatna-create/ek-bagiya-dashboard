Score Formula / अंक गणना सूत्र
Work Score /25: ((Sanction & Sipri Approved Works ÷ Target) × 15) + ((Sipri Suitable Sites ÷ Target) × 10)
Wage Score /50: Total Wage Expenditure ÷ Target Wage Amount × 50
Material Score /25: Total Material Expenditure ÷ Target Material Amount × 25
Overall Achievement /100: Work Score + Wage Score + Material Score
Remaining Wage: Wage Sanctioned − Wage Booked Since Inception
Remaining Material: Material Sanctioned − Material Booked Since Inception
FY metrics: 2025-2026 = Previous/last FY booked values; 2026-2027 = current FY booked values from NREGA sheet.
